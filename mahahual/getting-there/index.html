How to get to Mahahual

Here is how to get to Mahahual, a small beach town on the southern Caribbean coast of Quintana Roo. Most visitors fly into Chetumal or Tulum, about 2 hours away by car, or Cancún or Mérida, about 4 hours away. The last stretch is the same for everyone: Highway 307 to the Mahahual turnoff, then a paved road east to the sea.

The nearest airports

Chetumal

Chetumal is the closest airport. The drive is about 140 km and takes about 2 hours. There is no direct bus from the airport to Mahahual: take a taxi to the ADO bus station in the city and continue by bus, or book a private transfer. Transfer companies run this route; book ahead and give them your flight number so they can track a delay.

Tulum

Tulum's international airport is also about 2 hours away by car. You drive south on Highway 307 to the Mahahual turnoff.

Cancún and Mérida

Both airports are about 4 hours away by car. From Cancún the route is simple: Highway 307 south the whole way to the turnoff. From Mérida, plan the route on a map app before you set off.

Driving to Mahahual

From Cancún, Playa del Carmen or Tulum, follow Federal Highway 307 south. From Chetumal or Bacalar, take Highway 307 north. Either way, turn east at the junction signed for Mahahual and Costa Maya, at El Cafetal, a few kilometres south of the town of Limones. From the junction a paved road runs about 55 km east to the coast. There is a gas station before the turnoff at El Cafetal.

Mahahual is small and easy to find your way around. Apartments Tukano is one street back from the beach, on Calle Huauchinango at the corner of Calle Barracuda, directly behind Nacional Beach Club. Park in the covered spaces under the building.

The distances here are approximate. Check a current map app before you leave.

Getting to Mahahual by bus

ADO runs buses to Mahahual, but there are only a few departures a day, so check the timetable on ado.com.mx before you plan around one. The bus from Cancún takes about 5 hours. Buses also run from Playa del Carmen and from Chetumal.

There is no direct bus from Tulum: the trip changes buses at Limones. If you are arriving by bus, send us your arrival time on WhatsApp and we will help with directions from the bus stop.

Arriving on a cruise ship

The Costa Maya cruise port is just north of town, a short taxi ride from the centre. If you are staying on after a cruise, or meeting friends who arrive by ship, a taxi to the apartments is quick.

Getting around once you are here

You will not need a car in town. The beach, the malecón and the restaurants are all within walking distance of the apartments. A car is useful for day trips: inland to Bacalar or the Chacchoben ruins, or south to Xcalak. Our guide to things to do in Mahahual covers each one.

Driving down with family or a group? Our 3 bedroom apartments sleep up to 9 and have covered parking under the building.

Questions

How far is Mahahual from Cancún?

About 4 hours by car, south on Highway 307 and then east from the Mahahual turnoff. The ADO bus takes about 5 hours.

What is the closest airport to Mahahual?

Chetumal. The drive is about 140 km and takes about 2 hours. Tulum's airport is also about 2 hours away by car.

Is there a bus from Chetumal airport to Mahahual?

Not directly. Take a taxi to the ADO bus station in Chetumal and continue by bus, or book a private transfer.

Do I need a car in Mahahual?

Not in town. The beach, the malecón and the restaurants are all within walking distance of the apartments. A car is handy for day trips to Bacalar, Chacchoben or Xcalak.

Is there parking at Apartments Tukano?

Yes. There is covered parking under the building.
