How to get to Mahahual

Here is how to get to Mahahual, a small beach town on the southern Caribbean coast of Quintana Roo. You can come by Tren Maya to Limones-Chacchoben, about an hour by road from town, or fly into Chetumal, Tulum, Cancún or Mérida and drive. The last stretch by road is the same for everyone: Highway 307 to the Cafetal junction, then a paved road east to the sea.

By Tren Maya

The Tren Maya is the new passenger train around the Yucatán Peninsula. The nearest station to Mahahual is Limones-Chacchoben, about 63 km away, roughly an hour by road. Two trains a day run in each direction between Cancún Airport and Chetumal Airport, stopping at Limones-Chacchoben.

Approximate ride times to Limones-Chacchoben:

  • Cancún Airport: about 4 hours
  • Tulum Airport: under 2 hours
  • Chetumal Airport: about 1¼ hours
  • Bacalar: about 40 minutes

Timetables change, so check the official Tren Maya timetable before you plan around a train. If you are flying into Cancún or Tulum and would rather not drive, the train is the simpler option.

We can arrange your pickup from the Limones-Chacchoben train station. Send us your arrival time on WhatsApp.

Airports and drive times

Chetumal

Chetumal is the closest airport: about 140 km, or about 2 hours by car. From there you can drive, book a private transfer, or take the train to Limones-Chacchoben, where we can arrange your pickup.

Tulum

Tulum's airport is about 3 hours away by car, and under 2 hours by train to Limones-Chacchoben.

Cancún and Mérida

Cancún airport is about 4½ hours away by car, and so is Mérida. From Cancún Airport you can also take the train, about 4 hours to Limones-Chacchoben.

Driving directions

Take Federal Highway 307 to the Cafetal junction, just south of Limones. Turn east there and follow the paved road for about 55 km to the coast.

Mahahual is small and easy to find your way around. Apartments Tukano is one street back from the beach, on Calle Huauchinango at the corner of Calle Barracuda, directly behind Nacional Beach Club. The covered parking is at street level in the building. The distances here are approximate, so check a current map app before you leave.

The cruise port

The Costa Maya cruise port is about 3 km north of Mahahual, a few minutes by taxi. If you are meeting a ship, you can stay with us the night before or after, and one-night stays are welcome. See our page on getting from the Costa Maya cruise port to Mahahual.

Getting around town

You will not need a car in town. The beach, the malecón and the restaurants are all within walking distance of the apartments. A car is useful for day trips: inland to Bacalar or the Chacchoben ruins, or south to Xcalak. Our guide to things to do in Mahahual covers each one.

Driving down with family or a group? Our 3 bedroom apartments sleep up to 9 and have covered parking in the building.

Questions

What is the nearest train station to Mahahual?

Limones-Chacchoben on the Tren Maya. It is about 63 km from Mahahual, roughly an hour by road.

Can you pick me up from the train?

We can arrange your pickup from the Limones-Chacchoben train station. Send us your arrival time on WhatsApp.

What is the closest airport to Mahahual?

Chetumal. It is about 140 km away, about 2 hours by car. You can also take the train from Chetumal Airport to Limones-Chacchoben, about 1¼ hours.

How far is Mahahual from Cancún?

About 4½ hours by car from Cancún airport. By train, Cancún Airport to Limones-Chacchoben takes about 4 hours, and the station is about an hour by road from Mahahual.

Do I need a car in Mahahual?

Not in town. The beach, the malecón and the restaurants are all within walking distance of the apartments. A car is handy for day trips.

Is there parking at Apartments Tukano?

Yes. There is covered parking at street level in the building.
