From the Costa Maya cruise port to Mahahual

Getting from the Costa Maya cruise port to Mahahual takes a few minutes by taxi: the port is about 3 km north of town. Here is what to do with a day off the ship, and where to stay if you are meeting a ship or working at the port.

From the port into town

The Costa Maya cruise port is about 3 km north of Mahahual, a few minutes by taxi. Apartments Tukano is one street back from the beach, behind Nacional Beach Club. Once you are in town, the beach, the malecón and the restaurants are all within walking distance.

A day in Mahahual off the ship

The beach is the main event. The reef offshore shelters a shallow lagoon, so swimming and snorkelling inside the reef is calm and easy, and snorkel tours reach the reef on a short boat ride. Walk the malecón, the beachfront boardwalk with seafood restaurants and beach bars.

For food, our favourites are all close to the apartments: Café Colonial for breakfast, the taco stands right outside our building, Taquería El Pastorcito for tacos al pastor, and Camaroncito Caribeño for seafood on Calle Huauchinango. More in our guide to things to do in Mahahual.

Meeting a ship

If a friend or family member is a passenger or works on board, or you are arriving before a ship or staying on after one, you can stay in town a few minutes from the port. Our apartments are whole homes with a full kitchen, two bathrooms and A/C in every bedroom, so a group can arrive the night before and stay together.

One-night stays are welcome and can be booked online. The cleaning fee applies to every stay: MXN 500 for a two-bedroom, MXN 700 for a three-bedroom.

Working at the port

Crew, staff, contractors and suppliers who need a place for weeks or months can stay with us too. Stays of 7 nights or more get 10% off, monthly rates are available on request, and the maximum stay is 90 nights. Each apartment has a full kitchen, Wi-Fi and covered parking. See our long-stay page for details.

Questions

How far is the Costa Maya cruise port from Mahahual?

About 3 km north of town, a few minutes by taxi.

Can I stay in Mahahual to meet a ship?

Yes. Our apartments are one street back from the beach, a few minutes from the port, and you can book the night before or after the ship.

Can I stay just one night?

Yes. One-night stays are welcome and can be booked online. The cleaning fee is charged once per stay: MXN 500 for a two-bedroom, MXN 700 for a three-bedroom.

Do you have monthly rates for people working at the port?

Yes. Stays of 7 nights or more get 10% off, monthly rates are available on request, and the maximum stay is 90 nights.

Where can I eat near the beach?

Our favourite for breakfast is Café Colonial. There are taco stands right outside our building, Taquería El Pastorcito for tacos al pastor, and Camaroncito Caribeño for seafood.
