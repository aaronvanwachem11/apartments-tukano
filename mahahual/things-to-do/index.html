Things to do in Mahahual

The best things to do in Mahahual are simple: swim and snorkel in the calm water inside the reef, walk the malecón, take a boat to Banco Chinchorro and eat well. This is our guide from one street back from the beach, for guests staying a few days or a few weeks.

On the beach and in the water

Swim and snorkel inside the reef

The reef offshore shelters a shallow lagoon, so the water off town is usually calm and clear, and the beach is a short walk from your door. Snorkel tours reach the reef by boat in about ten minutes, and some guides take small groups out by kayak instead. Bring reef-safe sunscreen.

Diving

Mahahual sits on the Mesoamerican Reef, and the town's dive shops run trips to sites close to shore. For the bigger day out, see Banco Chinchorro below.

Paddleboarding and kayaking

Paddleboards and kayaks are an easy way to get out on the calm water inside the reef without booking a full tour.

Walk the malecón

The malecón is the beachfront boardwalk, lined with seafood restaurants and beach bars and within walking distance of the apartments. It is the place for an evening walk, and you can rent a bike to ride along it.

Take a boat to Banco Chinchorro

Banco Chinchorro is a protected reef atoll about 31 km offshore, reached on a full-day boat trip for snorkelling and diving. Calm weather matters, so try to book it early in your stay. Our Banco Chinchorro guide explains how the trips work.

Day trips from Mahahual

Xcalak

A fishing village about an hour's drive south, next to Xcalak National Reef Park, with unspoiled beaches.

Bacalar

Inland, Bacalar's lagoon is known as the Lagoon of Seven Colours. People go for the swimming, the kayaking and the colour of the water. It makes an easy day trip by car.

Chacchoben

The closest Maya ruins to Mahahual, about 70 km inland. The temples stand on a large raised platform, the Gran Basamento, in the jungle. Tours often pair it with a boat trip on the Bacalar lagoon.

Where to eat near the apartments

These are our own favourites, and all of them are an easy walk from the apartments.

For breakfast, our favourite is Café Colonial.

For tacos, you hardly need to go anywhere: there are many taco stands at street level right outside the building. When we want tacos al pastor, we go to Taquería El Pastorcito, another of our favourites.

For seafood, Camaroncito Caribeño is one of our favourite restaurants. It is on Calle Huauchinango, the same street as the apartments, and on the short walk there you will pass several excellent taco stands.

Questions

What is there to do in Mahahual?

Swim and snorkel in the calm water inside the reef, dive, paddleboard or kayak, walk the malecón, and take a boat trip to Banco Chinchorro. Xcalak, Bacalar and the Chacchoben ruins make good day trips.

Can you snorkel in Mahahual?

Yes. The reef shelters a shallow lagoon off town. Snorkel tours reach the reef by boat in about ten minutes, and some guides go out by kayak.

How do I visit Banco Chinchorro from Mahahual?

Book a full-day boat trip with a licensed operator in Mahahual. The crossing takes about 1.5 to 2 hours each way, and trips run only when the sea is calm enough.

Where can I eat near Apartments Tukano?

Our favourite for breakfast is Café Colonial. There are many taco stands right outside the building, Taquería El Pastorcito is our pick for tacos al pastor, and Camaroncito Caribeño, on our street, is one of our favourite seafood restaurants.

What day trips can I take from Mahahual?

Xcalak, a fishing village about an hour south; the Bacalar lagoon inland; and Chacchoben, the closest Maya ruins, about 70 km away. A car makes all three easy.
