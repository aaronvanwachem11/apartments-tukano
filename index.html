Your home base in Mahahual

Apartments Tukano is a building of eight furnished vacation apartments in Mahahual, on Mexico's Costa Maya. We sit one street back from the beach, right behind Nacional Beach Club, so the sand and the Caribbean are a couple of minutes' walk away.

Every apartment is a whole home: two or three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a full kitchen, a living and dining room and a private balcony. Air conditioning runs in every bedroom and living room, and there is covered parking under the building.

It works for families, groups of friends and longer winter stays. Book directly with us and you pay no booking-site fees.

Questions about staying in Mahahual

How far are the apartments from the beach?

Walk out the front door, cross the street and walk past Nacional Beach Club. You are on the sand in a couple of minutes.

How many people can stay in an apartment?

The two-bedroom apartments sleep up to 6 people. The three-bedroom apartments sleep up to 9. Every apartment has two bathrooms.

Do the apartments have air conditioning?

Yes. Every bedroom and every living room has air conditioning, and Wi-Fi is included.

Is there parking?

Yes. There is covered parking under the building.

How do I get to Mahahual?

Chetumal and Tulum airports are about 2 hours away by car. Cancún and Mérida airports are about 4 hours away. The Costa Maya cruise port is a short taxi ride north of town.
