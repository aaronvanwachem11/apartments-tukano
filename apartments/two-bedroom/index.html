Two-Bedroom · 2 apartments

2 bedroom apartment in Mahahual, one street from the beach

Our 2 bedroom apartment in Mahahual is a whole 100 m² home with two bathrooms, a full kitchen and a private balcony, one street back from the beach and directly behind Nacional Beach Club. The price includes up to 4 guests, with room for 6.

From MXN 1,550 / night

What's in the apartment

We have 2 two-bedroom apartments, all with the same layout and equipment.

  • 100 m², 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
  • Living and dining room
  • Private balcony
  • A/C in both bedrooms and the living room
  • Wi-Fi and a smart TV
  • Beach towels provided
  • Covered parking under the building
  • High chair and playpen on request

Beds

  • Main bedroom: king bed.
  • Second bedroom: queen bed plus a single.
  • Living room: sofa bed.

Kitchen

The full kitchen has a gas stove, microwave, coffee maker, blender, toaster and a drinking-water dispenser, plus cookware and tableware, so you can cook every meal at home if you like.

Prices and house rules

  • From MXN 1,550 per night, including up to 4 guests. Maximum 6 guests.
  • Extra guests: MXN 200 per person per night. Children count toward the maximum.
  • Cleaning fee: MXN 500 per stay.
  • 10% off stays of 7 nights or more. Monthly rates on request: see long stays.
  • Minimum stay 2 nights, maximum 90 nights.
  • Check-in 4:00 pm. Check-out 12:00 pm.
  • Pay 50% when you book and the balance 7 days before arrival. No security deposit.
  • Free cancellation up to 7 days before arrival. Inside 7 days, 50% is refunded. No refund for no-shows.
  • No smoking. No parties or events. Pets on request, with a refundable MXN 1,500 deposit.

Live prices for your dates are in the booking calendar.

Good to know before you book

  • No elevator. The building has stairs only. If stairs are hard for someone in your group, message us before you book.
  • Views. Suites 5, 6, 8 and 9 have ocean views. Suites 1, 4 and 7 have balcony views. Ask us which suites are free for your dates.
  • Season. We are open November through April and July through August. In 2027 we are closed May 1 to June 30 and September 1 to October 31.
  • Languages. We speak English and Spanish.

Location
