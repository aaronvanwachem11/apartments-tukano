Monthly rental in Mahahual for winter stays

Looking for a monthly rental in Mahahual for the winter? Our furnished apartments suit Canadian and American visitors staying 2 to 12 weeks, with a full kitchen, Wi-Fi and covered parking, one street back from the beach. We answer in English.

Long-stay pricing

  • 10% off any stay of 7 nights or more.
  • Monthly rates on request. For a month or longer, message us your dates and we will send you a monthly price.
  • One cleaning fee per stay, not per week: MXN 500 for a two-bedroom, MXN 700 for a three-bedroom.
  • Maximum stay 90 nights.
  • Pay 50% when you book and the balance 7 days before arrival. No security deposit.

Set up for living, not just a holiday

Every apartment is 100 m² with two bathrooms, a living and dining room and a private balcony. The full kitchen has a gas stove, microwave, coffee maker, blender, toaster, drinking-water dispenser, cookware and tableware, so cooking at home for weeks is easy.

There is Wi-Fi and a smart TV in every apartment, and A/C in every bedroom and the living room. If you drive down, your car stays in the covered parking under the building.

Season dates

We are open November through April and July through August. In 2027 we are closed May 1 to June 30 and September 1 to October 31. A winter stay can run anywhere from November to the end of April, up to 90 nights.

Which apartment?

The two-bedroom suits a couple or a small family, from MXN 1,550 a night for up to 4 guests. The three-bedroom gives room for visiting family or friends, from MXN 2,050 a night for up to 6 guests and a maximum of 9.

A few weeks in Mahahual

The beach, the malecón, the restaurants and the taco stands are all a walk from the door, so you will not need a car day to day. See how to get to Mahahual and our guide to things to do in Mahahual.

Before you book

Each extra guest beyond the included number is MXN 200 per night, and children count toward the maximum. Pets are welcome on request, with a refundable MXN 1,500 deposit. Send us your dates and the number of people on WhatsApp or by email to tukano@nym.hush.com, and we will reply with a price.
